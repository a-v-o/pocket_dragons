Warm the egg

An egg only hatches when it stays warm without getting too hot. Breathe fire in short bursts and keep the marker inside the bronze band.

Ready, Keeper?

Hatch the egg in as little time as you can.

Time0.0s Steady0%
  1. Hold the button, or the Space bar, to breathe fire. The egg warms up.
  2. Let go and it cools. Short bursts keep the marker steady.
  3. The bronze band drifts. Follow it to fill the hatching bar.
  4. Too hot burns the shell, and cold gusts pull the warmth down.
  5. Staying steady makes rarer dragons more likely.
